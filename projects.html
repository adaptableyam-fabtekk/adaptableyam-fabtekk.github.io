Projects

Recent work, start to finish

A selection of recent FABtekk projects across the UK — from complete oven relocations and rebuilds to precision parts fabricated in our Penrith workshop. Every job delivered by our own engineers, right first time.

Project / 01

Full oven relocation

Wales → Hull

A complete production oven moved between operating sites: decommissioned, sectioned and craned out in Wales, transported by low loader, then rebuilt, re-banded and recommissioned in Hull — one team, start to finish.

Decommission & lift outThe oven was stripped, split into sections and craned onto transport at the Welsh site.
TransportOven sections secured and moved cross-country by low loader.
Rebuild & re-bandSections repositioned, drives refitted and a new oven band installed and tensioned.
CommissioningLine tested and handed back to production in Hull.
Project / 02

Oven band installation

Supply and installation of a new woven wire oven band: the old band stripped out, the new band fed through the oven, spliced, tracked and tensioned — leaving a perfectly true running surface ready for production.

Project / 03

Full internal oven chamber replacement

Complete replacement of an oven's internal baking chamber: old plates and steelwork cut out, new chamber sections fabricated, fitted and welded in situ — returning the oven to as-new baking performance without replacing the whole machine.

Project / 04

Oven re-insulation

Strip-out of tired, compacted insulation and full re-insulation with new mineral wool and foil cladding — improving thermal performance, cutting fuel costs and making the bakery a cooler, safer place to work.

Project / 05

Burner tube refurbishment

Refurbishment of an oven's brick-lined burner tubes: old refractory linings repaired and rebuilt, and steelwork welded from the inside out — restoring safe, even combustion for years of service.

Project / 06

Divider parts fabrication

Precision replacement parts for bread dividers, made in our Penrith workshop — from fabricated and machined linkage assemblies to fully built-up drive units, finished and ready to fit.
