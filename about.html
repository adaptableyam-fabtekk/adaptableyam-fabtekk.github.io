About FABtekk

A passion for engineering since 2014

FABtekk Engineering was formed in 2014, built on a simple idea: understand the customer, and develop products and services that make the lives of those in production that little bit easier. More than a decade on, that idea still drives everything we do.

Today, FABtekk is widely respected as a quality service provider. We offer a dedicated, flexible workforce of engineers with varied skills, experience and knowledge of different working environments — providing services throughout the UK, with skills that are transferable anywhere in the world.

Paul Stephenson

Director

Over 30 years' experience in fabrication and product development in manufacturing, nationwide. Paul leads FABtekk with a commitment to delivering the best quality service to all — continuing to build on the company's reputation for service and innovation.
