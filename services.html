Services

Everything your production line needs

All aspects of production equipment covered — patisserie, bread and full production lines. Bread dividers, moulders, bake-off ovens, conveyors and more, serviced by engineers who know the food industry inside out.

SERVICE / 01

Fabrication & Spares

Design and fabrication of new components and spares from our Penrith workshop. Stainless steel work built to food-industry standards, from one-off replacement parts to complete assemblies.

SERVICE / 02

Preventative Maintenance

Structured maintenance contracts that keep your equipment running at its best. We identify wear before it becomes failure — reducing unplanned downtime and extending the life of your machinery.

SERVICE / 03

Emergency Call Out

When a line stops, every minute costs. Our engineers respond to breakdowns across the UK, diagnosing and repairing on site to get you back into production fast.

SERVICE / 04

Regular Divider Servicing

Bread dividers and moulders are our specialty. Scheduled servicing keeps scaling accurate and mechanisms reliable — protecting both product quality and output.

SERVICE / 05

Oven Cleaning & General Inspection

Thorough cleaning and inspection of production and bake-off ovens, inside and out. We report on condition as we go, so you know exactly where your ovens stand.

SERVICE / 06

Shift Cover

A dedicated, flexible workforce of engineers available to cover shifts in your plant — with the varied skills and experience to slot straight into your team.

SERVICE / 07

Installations & Decommissioning

Full installation and decommissioning of production machinery, including lifting, positioning, alignment and commissioning. Complete relocations delivered — like our recent full oven move from Wales to Hull.

SERVICE / 08

Refurbishments

Complete refurbishment of tired equipment — often a fraction of the cost of replacement, returning machinery to as-new condition and performance.

SERVICE / 09

Condition Reports

Independent, detailed condition reports on your plant and equipment — supporting maintenance planning, capital decisions and asset purchases.
